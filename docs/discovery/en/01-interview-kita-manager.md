# Interview: Kita manager

Name: ______________________  Date: ____________  Interviewer: ______________________

About 60 minutes. Ask, listen, write down their words. Show the app on a tablet where it helps ("is this how you would do it?"). There are no wrong answers: what matters is how the Kita really works today.

## 1. The Kita

- How many Children, Groups and Staff members (full-time, part-time, trainees, volunteers)?
- Opening hours? Early and late care, closing days, summer closure?
- Which federal state is the Kita in? (Decides the holidays, and maybe rules on how long data is kept.)

## 2. A normal day

- Walk me through a normal day, from the first Child arriving to the last one leaving.
- Where is information written down today (paper folders, lists, spreadsheets, a messenger, memory)?
- What goes wrong or takes too long?

## 3. Child profiles and safety

- What do you record about each Child today? Anything the app does not have yet?
- Allergies and medical conditions: who needs to know, and how do they find out now?
- Who may pick a Child up, and how do Staff check it at the door?
- Consents (photos, excursions, data protection): on paper? How do you see who has withdrawn one?
- Who should be allowed to change Child profiles? Only you, or also a deputy?

## 4. Groups, tasks and the calendar

- How are Groups organised? Do Children or Staff move between Groups during the day or year?
- How are tasks shared in a Group today (who buys craft supplies, who calls a Parent)?
- Which dates matter: Events, closing days, school holidays, Birthdays, parents' evenings? Who should be reminded, and how long before?

## 5. Attendance, Absences, Incidents, emergencies

- How do you know today who is here right now? Who checks Children in and out?
- How are Absences (sick, holiday) reported, and by whom?
- Accidents and Incidents: how are they recorded and followed up today? Who must be informed?
- In an evacuation or fire drill, how do you know who is in the building? Would a printed list per Group help?

## 6. Data protection and records

- How long must you keep data about a Child who has left? (The app keeps deleted profiles in an archive for 12 months for now.)
- Has a Parent ever asked to see, or to delete, the data you hold? How was it handled?
- Who, outside the Kita, may see any of the data (carrier, youth welfare office, therapists)?

## 7. People and devices

- Who would use the app: you, your deputy, each Teacher, trainees? Anyone who should only see the calendar (a Guest)?
- Which devices would they use: Kita tablets, PCs, their own phones?
- How comfortable is the team with computers? Who could help the others?

## 8. Priorities

- If the app did only three things well, which would they be?
- Is there anything in the app that you would not use, or that worries you?
- What would make you say "this was worth it" after three months?

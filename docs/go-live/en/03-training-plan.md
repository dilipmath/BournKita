# Training plan

Two short sessions on the Kita's own tablets, in the week before go-live, with made-up data on the server (as in the acceptance test) or, after go-live, with the real data and great care. Each participant uses their own account.

## Session 1: Every Staff member (60 minutes, groups of up to 6)

| Minutes | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Why the app; what stays on paper (see go-live checklist, "Paper fallback") | Talk |
| 10 | Log in, the menu with your name (language, log out), the bar at the bottom and the Group tabs, add the app to the home screen; forgotten password; automatic logout after 30 minutes | Everyone on their tablet |
| 15 | The Group's day: Check in, Check out, Undo, Absent; the Alerts marker | Each checks in and out two Children |
| 10 | A Child's profile: allergies, food, who may pick up | "Is Tom Krause allowed to pick up Lena?" |
| 5 | Report an incident | Each reports one made-up incident |
| 5 | The emergency list | Each opens it once; one is printed |
| 5 | Task board and Calendar, briefly; Reminders | Each adds one Task, moves it |
| 5 | Feedback; questions; hand out Cards 1 and 2 | |

## Session 2: Admins (90 minutes)

After session 1.

| Minutes | What |
|---|---|
| 15 | Staff accounts: invite, Roles, Groups, deactivate; at least one active Admin |
| 30 | A new Child profile from start to end: Group, allergies, Parents (and Siblings), Emergency contacts, pick-up persons with photo, Consents, photos |
| 10 | Withdrawn photo Consent; Child leaves → Archive → Restore; the purge after 12 months |
| 10 | Incident log and Follow-ups; the Audit log |
| 10 | Data-protection requests: export and permanent deletion (on a made-up Parent!) |
| 10 | Holidays import; the server's warning emails and the runbook; who to call |
| 5 | Questions; hand out Card 3 |

## Afterwards

- Note who took part: ______________________ (session 1) · ______________________ (session 2), date ____________.
- Staff who start later get session 1 from an Admin, with the manual and the cards.
- Repeat a short version after a big update (see `05`).

# Test script

For the person running the session (the facilitator). About 30 minutes per participant. Test with 3 to 5 Kita staff, one at a time, at least one who is unsure with computers. A second person takes notes on the observation sheet (04), if possible.

## Before

- The test stack is running with made-up data (see the README); a Teacher account is ready for the participant.
- The tablet shows the login page. Charged, sound off.
- One observation sheet (04) per participant.

## Introduction (2 minutes; read out)

> Thank you for helping. We are testing the app, not you: if something is hard, the app is at fault, and that is exactly what we want to find. Please think aloud: say what you are looking for, what you expect, what surprises you. I won't help while you try, but you can stop any task. All names in the app are made up. We take notes but no recordings. You can stop at any time.

## Tasks

Read each task out; do not name buttons or screens. Note the time, where they hesitate, and whether they finish without help.

| # | Task (read out) | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Please log in. Your email and password are on this card." | The Monkeys' Today view shows (a Teacher with one Group); the Kita overview for the Kita manager |
| 2 | "You work with the Monkeys today. Open their page." | The Monkeys' Today view shows |
| 3 | "Which Children have an allergy or illness you must watch out for?" | They name Lena (peanuts) and Noah (asthma) |
| 4 | "Emil has just arrived. Note that he is here." | Emil is checked in |
| 5 | "A man says he is collecting Lena: Tom Krause. Is he allowed to?" | They find Tom Krause under who may pick up |
| 6 | "Lena's mother calls: Lena is sick tomorrow and the day after. Note it." | An Absence for Lena, two days, reason sick |
| 7 | "Noah fell off the slide and grazed his knee. You put a plaster on and told his father. Note it." | An Incident for Noah is saved |
| 8 | "There's a fire drill. Get the list of who is here." | The Emergency list opens |
| 9 | "Note that someone needs to buy glue sticks, and that you are starting on it." | A new Task, moved to Doing |
| 10 | "Put a puppet show next Friday at 10 o'clock into the Monkeys' calendar." | The Event shows next Friday at 10:00 |
| 11 | "Something seems wrong to you. Tell the developer." | Feedback is sent |
| 12 (Admins only) | "Mia's grandmother can now pick her up too: Erika Schmidt, phone 030 555 1234. Add her." | A Pick-up person for Mia |

## After the tasks (5 minutes)

Ask, and write down their words:

1. What was easy? What was hard?
2. Was anything missing, or anything you would never use?
3. Were the buttons and texts big enough? Could you read everything?
4. Would you use this every day instead of paper? Why (not)?
5. On a scale from 1 (very hard) to 5 (very easy): how easy was the app overall?

## Afterwards

- Thank them. Reset the test data if needed (remove what they added, or restart the test stack).
- Fill in the rest of the observation sheet straight away.

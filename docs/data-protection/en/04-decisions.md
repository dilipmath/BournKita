## 4. Retention periods (see 01, section 6)

| Data | Today in the app | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Archived Child profiles (deleted, can be restored) | 12 months, then deleted for good | ☐ 12 months ☐ other: ____ months |
| Attendance and Absences of current Children | Kept while the Child is at the Kita | ☐ as now ☐ delete after ____ months |
| Incidents | With the Child | ☐ as now ☐ keep ____ years (e.g. for the accident insurer) |
| Photos after photo Consent is withdrawn | Hidden, kept until the Child is purged | ☐ as now ☐ delete them at once |
| A Deletion request by one Parent | Deletes all their Children and the Incident records | ☐ as now ☐ keep the Children while another Parent remains ☐ keep Incidents for ____ years |
| Tasks and Events | No limit | ☐ as now ☐ delete ____ months after they are done or over |
| Audit log | No limit | ☐ as now ☐ delete entries after ____ years |
| Feedback | No limit | ☐ as now ☐ delete after ____ months |
| Staff accounts of people who left | Deactivated, kept | ☐ as now ☐ delete after ____ years |
| Server logs | No limit set | ☐ keep ____ days |
| Nightly backups kept | the last 14 (about two weeks) | ☐ 14 ☐ other: ____ |
| Weekly off-site backups kept | the last 8 (about two months) | ☐ 8 ☐ other: ____ |

Every departure from "as now" becomes a ticket; the archive period and the number of backups kept are settings only.
